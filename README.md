## 🚀 Featured Projects

<details open>
<summary><b>🔮 Delivia</b></summary>
<br/>

Delivia reimagines food delivery for people on the move — instead of delivering to a fixed address, it delivers directly to a **moving vehicle**, tracking the car in real time and coordinating handoff with the delivery rider on the fly.

The core engineering challenge is real-time coordination — continuously tracking a moving target and dynamically re-routing the delivery rider to intercept it, rather than the traditional fixed drop-off model.

[View Repo](https://github.com/existsrarely/delivia)
